3.0 APPLICATIONS

Designed for austere environments, the ZEPHYR station provides critical telemetric data bridging the gap between isolated environmental vectors and centralized analytical matrices. Primary deployment scenarios encompass remote agricultural monitoring, extreme-weather advanced warning perimeters, and isolated infrastructural health assessments where conventional power and cellular topologies are unavailable or unreliable.
